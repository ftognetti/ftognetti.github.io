### Focaccia in the style of panettone

---

Basically I used the ingredients from Panettone but the preparation of a focaccia. 
The texture is more focaccia-y/cakey but the taste should get pretty close. 

This is a bit of an experiment of mine, since I love panettone but its preparation is 3 days long and includes several hours of kneading by hand and precise timing; I did it once and it broke me.

Anyhow, enjoy!
