### I didn't feel like accidentally killing my celiac friend, so I didn't make a focaccia

---

This is an amazing italian classic: Farinata di ceci (or cecina depending on who you ask) is made by 
dissolving chickpea flour and salt in water and EVO oil, letting it rest for at least a 
dozen hours and baking it in a tray for around half an hour.
The result is this spongy tasty savory cake-thing that I like quite a lot (and doesn't risk murdering your
celiac friends)
Enjoy! <3
