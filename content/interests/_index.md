### PhD Project: Polyhedral semantics of modal logic

Exploration of the connections between topology, geometry and logic, 
in particular looking for results regarding expressivity, completeness and 
soundness of polyhedral topological semantics for modal logic, and a study 
of Coalgebraic polyhedral semantics.

### Personal project: A (higher) categorical account of higher order modal logic

Defining and finding suitable models for higher order modal logic and its interaction 
with modal type theory
